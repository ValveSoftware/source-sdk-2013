Welcome to PASS Fortress

One night at the Brickyard warehouse, Merasmus the Wizard stumbled upon an old TV with mysterious, multi-dimensional powers. He decided to refine it, while concocting a plan.

The following day, Soldier, Demoman and Medic snooped around the warehouse. Soldier and Demo were captivated by the 'JACK', a small but powerful bomb. Medic was looking for things to use in his experiments. Merasmus found the trio, and wanted revenge on Soldier for having his castle turned into a raccoon sanctuary.

The three mercenaries now find themselves travelling to different dimensions, dealing with Merasmus's Multi-Dimensional Bloodsport.
