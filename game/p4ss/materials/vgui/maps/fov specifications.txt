For the INSTAX Wide, set your fov to 63.2 for 16:9 aspect ratios. 
When putting the image in the Photoshop template, scale it (proportionally) until the top and bottom bounds of the image match the top and bottom bounds of the image frame.

For the Polaroid, set your fov to 71.1 for 16:9 aspect ratios.
When putting the image in the Photoshop template, do the same as described for the INSTAX Wide.
